Deferred the imports that no module-level statement reads, cutting the
cost of ``import importlib_metadata`` by about 30% on Python 3.15 and 3
to 8% on earlier Pythons.

Python 3.15 honors the new ``__lazy_modules__`` declaration (PEP 810) and
so defers ``contextlib``, ``email``, and two of the package's own modules;
earlier Pythons ignore the declaration and keep importing them eagerly.
The gain they do see comes from ``platform`` and ``compat.py312``, which
moved into the functions that read them.
